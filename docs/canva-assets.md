Design reference: Hotels Business Website.pdf supplied by the owner, exported
from https://www.canva.com/design/DAHT60VgeCo/XWV0TDQsfj0u-Hy7GaIc3g/view

The backgrounds, original ANCA Organics logo, quality symbol, original value icons, and vanilla
photos in public/images/canva are original assets from the ZIP supplied by the
project owner (Element canva-20261007T033602Z-1-001.zip).

The displayed ANCA logo and three value icons use the replacement transparent
PNGs supplied by the owner: Hotels Business Website (3).png is the white ANCA
logo, (2).png is Transparency, (1).png is Consistency, and the unnumbered PNG
is Quality. They are copied unchanged to the corresponding *-hd.png files.

The Vanilindo mark, Indonesia map, Papua community photo, ocean texture,
social icons, and larger PNG versions of the vanilla photos were taken from
the owner's public Canva design. The eight WOFF2
fonts in public/fonts/canva are the font files used by that same design:
Poppins, Coco Gothic, Catchy Mager, Lora, and Glacial Indifference. The website serves these files
locally and does not depend on the Canva editor or external font requests.

Canva assets are visual defaults. Images uploaded through the admin panel
take priority over the corresponding default asset. Published products and
articles remain database driven. The Canva draft's placeholder article text
is replaced by an empty state until an article is published.

The PDF has a 1024.5pt-wide page, equivalent to the 1366px Canva artboard.
The desktop site fills the screen, including the requested 1920px width.
Container-relative cqw dimensions preserve the PDF's element and section
proportions at that width. Each major section was measured from the PDF;
the 630px footer at the base width is shorter than the former 768px version.
The mobile layout stacks content and uses an accessible menu so text remains
readable on phones.

The public site is one continuous page: Home, About Us, Products, Blog, then
the brown Contact footer. The sticky header and footer navigation use section
anchors, with enough scroll offset to keep section headings below the header.
The former standalone page URLs redirect to those anchors. Product and article
detail pages remain available; their navigation returns to the matching section.
The product catalog keeps its own pagination and section anchor. Blog posts use
a horizontal, swipeable carousel with a centered image/title/description card
and triangular previous/next controls, matching the Canva blog layout. Published
posts open their administrator-provided HTTP(S) links directly; legacy articles
without external links retain their detail pages.
